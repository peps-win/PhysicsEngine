1. Build the program to WASM
    -cargo build --target wasm32-unknown-unknown --release
2. Copy the fresh build into web_build
    -Copy-Item target\wasm32-unknown-unknown\release\PhysicsEngine.wasm web_build\ -Force
3. Zip the web_build
    -Compress-Archive -Path * -DestinationPath ../physicsengine_web.zip -Force
4. Upload it to itch.io
